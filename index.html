Big Discoveries Begin Here.

Every day brings new opportunities to learn, play, create and grow. Our Pre-Primary Wing encourages curiosity, confidence and a love of learning through meaningful experiences.

See how children learn

Our approach

Learning through play, with a purpose

Our programme follows the National Education Policy 2020 and the National Curriculum Framework for the Foundational Stage, with attention to Foundational Literacy and Numeracy.

Learning is child-centred and inclusive, and it respects each family’s culture and values. The aim is a safe, caring place where children build good habits, kind behaviour and confidence.

How lessons come alive

Ideas are taught through themes and everyday context, so children understand them by doing.

  • Storytelling
  • Games
  • Art and craft
  • Music and movement
  • Exploration
  • Hands-on activities
  • Group work

Icebreakers and circle time help new children settle, speak up and listen. A balanced day mixes learning, movement, creative work, social time and rest.

What children develop

Six areas of growth

By the end of these years we hope to see children who communicate with confidence, use their hands and bodies well, express themselves creatively and begin to solve problems on their own.

Language and literacy
Listening, speaking, reading and writing. Rhymes, storytelling, singing, recitation, public speaking, newspaper reading and discussion.
Numeracy and thinking
Observation, questioning, shapes, sizes, patterns, sorting, grouping, sequencing and comparison.
Physical development
Sports, dance, music and outdoor play.
Socio-emotional and ethical
My Best Buddy, Sharing & Caring, Mother’s Day, Labour Day, Grandparent’s Day and the Fruits and Vegetable Market.
Aesthetic and cultural
Painting, drawing, craft, community helpers and festivals.
Learning habits
Circle time, curiosity, asking questions and taking part.

Day care

A calm second home after class

For working parents, the Day Care Centre looks after children in a safe, nurturing setting with indoor and outdoor play, creative expression and age-appropriate activities.

Qualified caregivers, extended care, daily updates to parents, first aid and medical assistance, and air-conditioned rooms with CCTV surveillance.

Inclusive education

The PRERNA Wing

Jaypee Public School is an inclusive school. Children learn at different speeds and in different ways, and every class makes room for that.

The PRERNA Wing is the school’s inclusive education wing. Trained teachers work with children who need extra help with learning, so that each child can build confidence and take a full part in school life, with teachers and parents working together.
